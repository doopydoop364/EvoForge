Game artwork sources
====================

Flappy Bird
-----------
The six flappy-*.png files are copied byte-for-byte from sourabhv/FlapPyBird,
revision 95af73307f7c05f2ba8f208e911097cbb56b689a:
https://github.com/sourabhv/FlapPyBird/tree/95af73307f7c05f2ba8f208e911097cbb56b689a/assets/sprites

These are the familiar Flappy Bird game sprites, preserved without recoloring,
resizing, or AI regeneration. Flappy Bird and its original artwork are credited
to Dong Nguyen / .GEARS. The supplying project's MIT notice is retained in
FLAPPYBIRD-LICENSE.txt; this notice does not assert ownership of the original
commercial game's artwork or relicense it as EvoForge CC0 artwork.

Classic Nokia Snake II
---------------------
The seventeen nokia-*.png files are copied byte-for-byte from HelpfulSheep's
browser recreation of Nokia Snake II:
https://www.helpfulsheep.com/snake/
https://www.helpfulsheep.com/snake/scripts/script.js

The supplying page identifies itself as a remake. These are its monochrome
Nokia-style sprites, not a verified extraction from Nokia phone firmware.
Nokia's original game is credited to Nokia / Taneli Armanto; the supplying
browser recreation is credited to HelpfulSheep. No CC0 license is claimed for
these third-party PNGs. The flat SVG LCD backgrounds use the recreation's
#b2bd08 display color.

SPRITE-SOURCES.json records the exact source URL and SHA-256 of every PNG.
All sprite PNGs are used unchanged. Directions and animation frames select
among the supplied files; transformations happen only during presentation.
Training rules, observation space, rewards, and saved checkpoints are unchanged.
